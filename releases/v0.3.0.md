# Romanian Monetary Dynamics v0.3.0

**Reference-mode closure and Accounting Spine recovery boundary milestone**

RMD v0.3.0 freezes the first public scientific-core snapshot in which all ten required aggregate reference modes are observed while the incomplete bilateral Accounting Spine is explicitly consolidated into an evidence-triggered recovery hold.

## What changed since v0.2.0

### Reference modes: 10 / 10 observed

- promoted `sectoral_financial_positions` from the final 9/10 blocker to an observed aggregate quarterly reference mode;
- used a separately preregistered ECB national-QFA consistency rule and official threshold evidence rather than relaxing a failed tolerance after the fact;
- retained the historical 9/10 terminal snapshot as provenance;
- kept the aggregate reference-mode promotion separate from bilateral Accounting Spine completion.

### Accounting Spine recovery boundary

- retained **F3** as the only canonically complete stock-and-flow instrument;
- retained **F2** as incomplete: F2M is materialized, while F21/total F2 remain blocked by the missing Direct-Investment currency-and-deposits subinstrument;
- retained the **F4** unconditional partial core while rejecting an invalid Eurostat-financial-accounts minus ECB-BSI bridge for the BNR counterpart allocation;
- executed preregistered OECD counterpart selective-reopen cycles for **F5, F6, F7 and F8**;
- stopped each F5-F8 Stage 2 before rank admission when the frozen source-reconciliation gates failed;
- recorded a terminal Accounting Spine recovery state: every incomplete instrument has an explicit frozen boundary and reopen trigger, no selective reopen is active, and no unconditional accounting-recovery task remains open.

### Reproducibility and scientific governance

- added retained-vintage OECD/ECB/ESA diagnostics for topology, semantics, precision, exact-decimal arithmetic and window stability;
- made release-version state and Accounting Spine terminal-recovery state explicit cross-registry CI invariants;
- expanded Scientific CI with instrument-specific reopen/execution audits and the terminal Accounting Spine recovery audit;
- kept repeated unchanged-source probing from being counted as scientific progress;
- preserved immutable v0.1.0 and v0.2.0 release identities.

## Scientific status at the v0.3.0 snapshot

## What v0.3.0 does not claim

This release is **not**:

- a complete bilateral F2-F8 Accounting Spine;
- a complete endogenous System Dynamics model;
- a calibrated macroeconomic forecast;
- a validated policy simulator;
- evidence that candidate relationships are causally identified;
- evidence that unresolved accounting cells are zero;
- permission to open protected holdouts, refit frozen mechanisms or relax preregistered evidence gates.
